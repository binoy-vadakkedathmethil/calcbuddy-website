Privacy Policy

How CalcBuddy handles information when you use our application and website.

Effective Date: September 23, 2026

CalcBuddy ("we", "our", or "us") operates the CalcBuddy mobile application and website.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use CalcBuddy.

1. Information We Collect

CalcBuddy does not currently collect, store, or require users to provide any personal information.

You can use CalcBuddy without creating an account or providing personal details such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password
  • Postal address
  • Government identification information

The calculations you perform in the app may be stored locally on your device to provide features such as calculation history. This information is not transmitted to our servers unless a particular feature explicitly states otherwise.

2. Calculator Data

CalcBuddy provides calculators such as BMI, Gold Loan, Home Loan, Car Loan, and other financial or general-purpose calculators.

Information entered into a calculator is used to perform the requested calculation.

We do not use calculator inputs to personally identify you.

Calculator results stored in your calculation history are intended to remain on your device.

3. Advertising

CalcBuddy may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google.

AdMob may collect and use information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • Approximate location
  • App interaction information
  • Diagnostic information

This information may be used to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising, subject to Google's policies and your device or account settings.

4. Third-Party Services

CalcBuddy may use third-party services including Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may process information according to its own privacy policies.

5. Internet Connection

Some CalcBuddy features may require an internet connection, particularly features that retrieve information from online services.

When such services are used, information may be transmitted to the relevant third-party service to provide the requested functionality.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by CalcBuddy. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

CalcBuddy is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through CalcBuddy, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

8. Your Choices

You may control certain advertising-related settings through your Android device and Google account settings.

You may also stop using the application or uninstall it at any time.

Locally stored application data may be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling it, subject to Android device behavior.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or CalcBuddy's privacy practices, please contact us through the contact information provided on the CalcBuddy website.
